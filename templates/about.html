About ShotGeek


The internet felt like an infinite world full of discoveries while I was growing up. I would spend hours “surfing the web,” exploring cool websites built around my niche interests and then telling my friends about them at school.

ShotGeek is an homage to that era, a corner of the internet that a younger version of me would’ve spent hours on, geeking out over basketball history, player trends, stats, and discovering hidden gems from every era of the NBA.

At its heart, ShotGeek is a basketball archive and companion app for exploring statistics, shot profiles, tendencies, and player comparisons for both current and retired NBA players.

ShotGeek is also an open-source project built with Django. Developers, designers, writers, and basketball fans from around the world have contributed to the project over the years.
